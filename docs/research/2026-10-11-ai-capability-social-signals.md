agents and editable deliverables. Repository descriptions show workflow and
integration capabilities, not independently established gains in model
intelligence or productivity.

Coverage is partial. GitHub Trending was directly inspected; Reddit evidence
comes from a secondary archive. No verified cross-platform share or repost
leaderboard was established.

## Capability evidence

The following descriptions come from the linked repositories' primary README
sources. They describe implementations and advertised capabilities, not
independent reproductions or controlled tests.

### Inspection and editable visual deliverables

- [REA](https://github.com/morluto/rea) connects MCP agents to local binary,
  JavaScript, Electron, and web inspection. Supported targets vary; this is
  not evidence of universal application access.
- [ArtCraft](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft) combines visual 2D/3D
  staging with AI image and video creation.
- [Diagram-design](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design) packages an
  agent workflow producing self-contained HTML/SVG across 44 diagram types.
  This is a workflow package, not a new underlying model.
- [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) renders
  HTML/CSS/media deterministically to MP4. It is not video diffusion.
- [Text-to-CAD](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad) produces parametric
  models with STEP, STL, 3MF, and GLB outputs and manufacturing checks.
  Those checks do not assure safe manufacture.

### Persistent context and specialized execution

- [Claude-mem](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem) carries observations
  and summaries into future agent context.
- [OpenRig](https://github.com/mvschwarz/openrig) supports persistent agent
  teams and handover.
- [Open-code-review](https://github.com/alibaba/open-code-review) specializes
  in structured review. Its efficiency benchmark claims are vendor-reported,
  not independently verified here.

### Resource and voice workflows

- [AirLLM](https://github.com/lyogavin/airllm) streams model layers to reduce
  VRAM requirements. Disk, host-memory, and speed costs were not verified.
- [VoiceStudio](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio) provides local voice
  cloning, voice design, dubbing, and transcription. Use requires appropriate
  permission and attention to separate model licenses.

Implementation material inspected in the original session included Claude-mem's
`src/services/context/ContextBuilder.ts`, OpenRig's
`packages/daemon/src/domain/context-packs/seat-recap-store.ts`, HyperFrames'
`packages/engine/src/index.ts`, AirLLM's
`air_llm/airllm/airllm_qwen4_exp.py`, VoiceStudio's
`docs/feature-catalog.md`, and Open-code-review's `internal/agent/grouping.go`.
Inspection does not establish runtime performance.

## Bottom line

Within this limited sample, specialized persistent agents and editable
deliverables attracted the strongest supported attention. This is not evidence
of market-wide growth, all-platform leadership, or proven productivity gains.
